Policies

Clear Terms,
No Surprises.

Everything about deposits, cancellations, travel and more — spelled out plainly.

A 30% non-refundable deposit is required to reserve your date. Your date is officially reserved once the deposit has been received. The remaining balance is due the day before the event.

Accepted payment methods:

  • Zelle
  • Venmo
  • Cash App
  • Cash
  • Credit / debit cards (applicable fee)

Bank transfer is not currently accepted.

  • The deposit is non-refundable.
  • If the bride cancels, the full amount is due according to the current policy.
  • Cancellation should be made at least one month in advance.
  • If the wedding is postponed, the deposit is not refunded.
  • If Maritza cancels the service, the deposit is returned.
  • Date changes are allowed.
  • A date-change fee may apply (amount confirmed directly with Maritza).
  • If the wedding is postponed, the deposit is not refunded.

Clients have a 15-minute grace period. After 15 minutes, a fee of $10 for every additional 15 minutes of delay applies. If the delay affects the schedule of other clients, GlamLove will determine whether services can still be completed.

  • Adding a person: the additional service is added to the final balance.
  • Removing a person: the removed service/person remains payable, per current policy.
  • Changing the time: may be adjusted if there is no conflicting booking.
  • Changing venue/location: the travel fee may change depending on the new location.
  • Changing hairstyle or makeup look: no additional fee currently indicated.

Travel fees apply depending on location (see the Bridal page for confirmed fees). Parking and hotel expenses are paid by the client. Out-of-state events require the client to cover applicable travel expenses.

GlamLove may use client photographs and Before & After content for portfolio and marketing purposes. Wedding photographs may be published with client authorization — you'll be asked for optional photo consent as part of your booking request.

Clients must inform GlamLove of any known allergies or product sensitivities before their appointment. This is asked as part of the booking request form.

Before Your Appointment

How To Prepare

Skin

  • —Arrive with clean skin, without makeup
  • —Facial recommended 1–2 weeks before the event
  • —Avoid intensive treatments 2 weeks before
  • —Exfoliate 2 days before
  • —Wash and moisturize the night before

Hair

  • —Hair should be clean and dry
  • —Wash the night before, using shampoo only
  • —Bring extensions if needed
  • —Bring event hair accessories
