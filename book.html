✦ Important

Policies & Terms of Service

Before submitting, please review our core service terms. Full policy details are always accessible on our Policies Page:

  • 30% Non-Refundable Deposit: Required to secure and officially lock your date on Maritza's calendar. The remaining balance is due the day before your event.
  • Cancellation & Rescheduling: Cancellations must be made at least 30 days in advance. Deposits are non-refundable; date changes are subject to availability.
  • Punctuality & Schedule: A 15-minute grace period is provided. Beyond 15 minutes, a $10 fee per additional 15 minutes applies to protect your timeline.
  • Travel & Parking: Applicable travel fees are confirmed prior to deposit. Parking and hotel fees (if needed) are covered by the client.

You must read and agree to the Policies & Terms of Service to submit your request.
