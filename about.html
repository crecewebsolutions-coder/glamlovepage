About GlamLove

An Artist For
Your Wedding Morning.

Since 2009, GlamLove Salon By Maritza has been part of Arizona's most meaningful mornings — quietly, carefully, and with a lot of heart.

The Story

Beauty Rooted in Connection.

GlamLove Salon By Maritza began with a simple belief: that getting ready should feel just as special as the wedding itself. What started as a passion for hair and makeup has grown into a full bridal beauty studio, trusted by brides across Arizona for nearly a decade.

Every appointment is treated as what it is — a once-in-a-lifetime morning. Maritza works closely with each bride to design a look that feels like them, not a trend, so the finished result photographs beautifully and feels effortless all day long.

The Approach

Full-Service,
On Your Terms.

Hair and makeup are designed together — not as separate services — so your entire bridal party looks cohesive, radiant, and timeless in every single photo.

There's no minimum party size, and no getting-ready location is off the table: homes, luxury hotels, venues, Airbnbs, bridal suites, and photography studios throughout Arizona are all welcome.
